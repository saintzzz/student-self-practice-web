# Edu-app research - Week 48, 2026

Scope: STEM/science & English cross-subject methods (rotation item 5),
per W47's next-week note. How the best products teach science-in-English
(CLIL), which science pedagogies have real effect sizes, and an audit of
our khoa-hoc track against them.
Method: peer-reviewed syntheses and randomized studies (Slavin JRST 2014,
Granger et al. Science 2012, POE meta-analysis 2024, CLIL meta-analyses),
third-party efficacy reports (WestEd), and VN market context (IOE).
Correlational and self-report findings are labeled as such.

## Landscape: what the best products actually do

| Product / method | Signature mechanic | Evidence / scale |
|---|---|---|
| **Mystery Science** | Anchor every unit in a puzzling real-world phenomenon ("Why do woodpeckers peck?"), hands-on investigation, then explanation | Phenomena-driven design; ESSA Level IV only - logic model exists, NO efficacy study met inclusion (LearnPlatform 2024). Strong teacher adoption is engagement evidence, not learning evidence |
| **BrainPOP (Jr)** | Short animated movie per topic -> quiz; characters narrate concepts | Independent pre/post studies: knowledge-transfer ES=1.00 elementary (N=418), SAT-10 +13 science / +8 vocabulary scale-score points vs control; animated-movie RCT N=1335 -> science thinking skills + motivation up |
| **Legends of Learning** | 2000+ short standards-aligned games; play >=2x/week | WestEd 2023 (N=14k, g5+g8): higher usage associated with up to +23 percentile points on state science test - ASSOCIATIONAL, not causal. Vanderbilt RCT: ~half a letter grade in 3 weeks of classroom use |
| **Generation Genius** | NSTA-produced videos + DIY activities + quizzes | No studies met Evidence-for-ESSA inclusion. WestEd survey N=439 g4 is self-report only (92% "helped me learn") - engagement signal, no measured outcome |
| **POE (Predict-Observe-Explain)** | Child commits to a prediction -> sees the observation -> reconciles/explains | Meta-analysis of 35 studies: g=0.979 overall; ELEMENTARY band g=1.015 - the strongest measured effect we surveyed. Prediction-commitment + explanation is the active mechanism, not hands-on kit |
| **CLIL (science taught IN English)** | Content subject delivered through L2 | Meta-analysis (9 studies): vocabulary g=0.84 (CI 0.56-1.11) vs EFL-only; but listening/reading NULL. Greek primary RCT: L2-English science lessons -> significant vocab (p=.001) AND content gains (p=.000). Spanish g3 study: science CLIL materials carry more abstract/technical vocab and are HARDER than regular English class - productive vocab still grew significantly |
| **Slavin best-evidence synthesis** (2014) | Reviewed 23 controlled studies of elementary science programs | Science kits/manipulatives ALONE: ES=+0.02 (null). Inquiry + teacher PD: +0.36. Tech (video/computer) + cooperative learning: +0.42 (small studies). Science-reading integration: positive. Materials without pedagogy don't move outcomes |
| **Dual coding / imagery** | Pair word with picture; child draws the concept | Small randomized studies: provided-picture pairing mixed (one null), image CREATION significantly beat word-only on science terms; delayed 2-week recall favored imagery groups |
| **IOE Science (VN)** | Science content rides the English exam; primary formats = picture-matching, True/False on pictures, word-order, listening | ioe.vn + format guides: 35 vong tu luyen self-practice ladder; our khoa-hoc program mirrors this niche - fact recall in English is the actual exam surface |

## Gaps ranked by expected impact

### G1. Science distractors are junk, not misconceptions - HIGH
Current distractors are silly fillers a child would never believe
("What do plants need to grow? -> candy, toys, shoes"), so items don't
discriminate and teach nothing on a miss. The POE/misconception
literature (g~1.0 elementary) says the learning happens when a
plausible wrong belief is committed to and then corrected. CR-137
proved the why-wrong feedback slot works on EN; science is where
documented K-5 misconceptions are richest (moon emits light, plants eat
soil, seasons = distance from sun, heavier falls faster).
-> IMPLEMENT this cycle (CR-144): misconception-calibrated distractors
with per-option "tai sao sai" feedback on every science MCQ, plus
generated feedback on classification items and a small fact-growth pass
authored misconception-first. Pure content + one field + one line in
the verdict path - the cheapest high-evidence item on the board.

### G2. No predict-first step - recall-only items - MEDIUM-HIGH
Every science item asks for the answer directly; nothing makes the child
commit to a prediction before the correct claim appears. POE's active
ingredient is exactly that commitment-then-reconcile cycle, and it fits
our format as a question-level wrapper (predict tap -> answer reveal ->
explain) without video or simulation.
-> File CR (predict-first science round, CR-145) for a future cycle -
needs a session-flow change and child-readable design, not a one-line
patch.

### G3. No imagery on science facts - MEDIUM
IOE's primary format is literally word<->picture matching, and imagery
studies favor pictures for delayed science-vocab recall - but the
strongest result is image CREATION, not provided pictures, and our
provided-image evidence is mixed. Our image pipeline exists; the cost
is asset production across 43+ facts plus non-concrete terms
(vertebrates, water cycle) that resist literal illustration.
-> Backlog - revisit when image cost drops; note the create-don't-show
twist (a "draw it" prompt may beat a provided image anyway).

### G4. Thin bank depth - MEDIUM
43 facts exhausts fast under adaptive practice. Growing the bank is
real authoring cost and must keep the CR-135 semantic guard; the
misconception-first authoring in G1 already adds facts where classic
misconceptions exist, which is the highest-value growth.
-> Absorbed into CR-144's scope; further bulk growth stays backlog.

### G5. Virtual lab / hands-on simulation layer - DROP
Slavin: kits and materials alone show ES=+0.02 - the investment is
large and the un-aided-mechanism evidence is null. If we ever build
interactives, they must carry a predict-explain wrapper (G2), which is
where the measured effect lives.
-> Drop for now; recorded so "add a simulation" doesn't resurface as a
default-good idea.

### G6. Phenomenon-anchored video units (Mystery Science analog) - DEFER
Highest content cost on the board (produced video/animation per
phenomenon) and Mystery Science itself has no included efficacy study.
BrainPOP's numbers are real but tied to a content library we can't
reproduce cheaply.
-> Defer; the G1/G2 items deliver the same pedagogy in text form.
