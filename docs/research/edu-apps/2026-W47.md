# Edu-app research - Week 47, 2026

Scope: gamification & retention mechanics (rotation item 3) + VN
competitor audit (item 4), per W46's next-week note. Which loops do the
highest-retention products actually run, which have real evidence, and
what should English Arena borrow vs deliberately refuse.
Method: official product blogs/docs (Duolingo engineering blog, VioEdu,
Monkey, Kyna), peer-reviewed studies (ClassDojo experiments), and
meta-analyses of gamification effects. Negative/mixed findings included.

## Landscape: what the best products actually do

| Product | Signature mechanic | Evidence / scale |
|---|---|---|
| **Duolingo** | Streak SEPARATED from daily goal (1 lesson extends streak, goal progress shown separately) - A/B test: +3.3% D14 retention, +10.5% daily learners on streak in 20 days. Streak Freeze (hold 2): +0.38% DAU, no extra days-off. Loss aversion kicks in at day 7 - most UX effort targets days 0-7 | Company A/B data, blog.duolingo.com |
| **ClassDojo** | Teacher-awarded points for behavior/skills | Mixed: engagement/on-task up (GBG study, ESL badges study - effect in grades 3-4, NOT 1-2). Inria review: no evidence on learning outcomes, flags potential extrinsic-motivation harm |
| **Blooket** | 27 self-paced modes + collectible "Blooks" - replay driver is collection, not speed. Solo/homework modes keep completion high | Teacher reviews (ditchthattextbook): top student pick, K-8 |
| **Gimkit** | Virtual economy - correct answers earn cash, kids CHOOSE how to invest (upgrades/power-ups). No speed scoring - strategy decides | Teacher reviews: deepest engagement for older kids |
| **Kahoot** | Speed-scored live quiz, leaderboard after each question | Critique: Classic's speed bonus lets fast guesses outscore careful answers; product added Accuracy Mode to compensate |
| **VioEdu** (VN) | Personalized daily missions (<30 min, tuned to entry test), kim cương points, leaderboards, instant feedback, "đấu trường" arenas (thách đấu 1v1 + mass events), đổi quà real-prize redemption | 5M+ students in arena events (vnexpress/vio.edu.vn) |
| **Monkey Junior** (VN) | Stars + coins -> sticker rewards, micro-games per lesson, fast flow | Market-leading VN pre-school EN app |
| **Kyna for Kids** (VN) | Micro-learning daily missions, Kani mascot guide, badges on correct, Cambridge-track lessons | 5000+ families at launch (eva.vn) |
| **Meta-analyses** | Gamification overall: small positive after publication-bias correction (achievement g=0.316, motivation g=0.183; prediction intervals include negative). Primary-school effect is the WEAKEST band (g=0.309 vs 1.015 secondary). Ranking/leaderboard + reward/award elements showed NEGATIVE motivation effects in one synthesis. Competition+ collaboration was the strongest combo for behavioral outcomes | Review of Educational Research 2025; Hacettepe K-12 meta; Springer EPR 2019 |

## Gaps ranked by expected impact

### G1. Server-truth streak + separated daily goal - HIGH
Our streak lives in localStorage: dies on device switch/browser clear,
invisible to parents/teachers, and our daily quests are the SAME event
that extends it (the coupling Duolingo proved harmful: -D14 retention
when goal gates streak). Duolingo's exact fix: streak = any 1 completed
round; goal = separate N-questions progress. `practice.results` +
`qb_attempt_events` already log the activity - pure derivation, no new
writes. Include the day-3/7 milestone moment (loss-aversion window)
using existing StreakBurst FX.
-> IMPLEMENT this cycle (CR-138, filed W46, carried over - evidence
reaffirmed and schema feasibility verified this week).

### G2. Live arena beyond EN (VioEdu đấu trường analog) - MEDIUM-HIGH
VioEdu's arenas drew 5M+ VN students; meta-analysis says competition
+ collaboration is the strongest behavioral combo. Our realtime race
infra exists but serves EN only; math-vi has ~1175 items and visual
puzzles now - a math arena is wiring, not new infra. Same-seed races
keep it fair; accuracy-first scoring avoids the Kahoot trap.
-> File CR (math-vi arena; team/coop variant noted for later).

### G3. Collectible depth pass - MEDIUM
Blooket's retention hook is collection (Blooks rarity/completeness).
We have the album + pet + home items - the hook exists, depth doesn't
(no rarity tiers, no completeness display). Marginal evidence is thin;
the mechanic only works when content volume justifies it.
-> Backlog - revisit after album content grows.

### G4. Teacher-awarded recognition (ClassDojo points) - MEDIUM
ClassDojo proves teacher-given points move on-task behavior but NOT
learning outcomes; the grades 1-2 null result and leaderboard/reward
negative-motivation signals argue caution. Also blocked on teacher
dashboard work. If built later, award effort/team points, never rank.
-> Backlog - needs teacher tools; note the design constraint.

### G5. Speed-scored competition - DROP
Kahoot Classic's speed bonus rewards fast guessing over careful
reasoning - wrong incentive for K-5. Our arena records timeMs; keep it
tiebreak-only, never the score.
-> Drop (recorded as a design guardrail, not a feature).

### G6. Real-prize redemption (VioEdu đổi quà) - DROP
Ops/logistics-heavy, extrinsic-reward risk per meta-analysis, and
school renewal doesn't need physical prizes.
-> Drop.

## Decisions this week (PO)

- **IMPLEMENT - CR-138** (carried from W46): server-truth streak +
  separated daily goal + weekend amulet + day-3/7 milestone moment.
  Highest-evidence retention item on the board; feasibility verified
  (results/qb_attempt_events already persist activity). This week's
  research added two refinements to its spec: (a) streak extends on ANY
  1 completed round - never gate it behind the daily-goal count
  (Duolingo's +3.3% D14 finding); (b) milestone surface at days 3/7
  reuses StreakBurst, no new assets.
- **File CR**: math-vi live arena (G2) - extends existing arenaLive
  infra to the math bank; accuracy-primary scoring, same-seed races.
- **Backlog**: collectible depth (G3), teacher recognition points (G4).
- **Drop**: speed-scored competition (G5), real-prize redemption (G6) -
  guardrails recorded so they don't resurface as "new ideas".
