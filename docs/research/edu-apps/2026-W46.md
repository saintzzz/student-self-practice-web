# Edu-app research - Week 46, 2026

Scope: K-5 math pedagogy + engagement mechanics from the strongest global
products. Goal: what English Arena's `math-vi` track should borrow next.
Method: public docs/blogs/research papers of each product.

## Landscape: what the best products actually do

| Product | Signature method | Evidence/scale |
|---|---|---|
| **ST Math** (MIND) | Spatial-temporal visual puzzles - teach visually FIRST, symbols LATER. Mastery-based (100% to advance), formative animated feedback, no language barrier | 1.3M+ students, strongest for underserved populations |
| **DreamBox** | Intelligent Adaptive Learning - watches STRATEGY not just right/wrong (e.g. building 37 as 3 tens vs 37 singles = different mastery). Virtual manipulatives, adapts within-lesson | K-8, school-deployed |
| **Zearn** | CPA (concrete→pictorial→abstract) + "precise digital feedback at the moment of misconception". Digital manipulatives mirror hands-on classroom | Top-rated curriculum, Eureka-aligned |
| **Beast Academy** (AoPS) | Comic-style narrative + genuinely HARD puzzles (productive struggle). Rigorous, not drill | 1M+ books sold |
| **Prodigy** | Full game layer (battle/quests) where every action = a math question. Teacher reports | JHU study: usage correlates w/ achievement gains |
| **IXL** | Massive skill tree, adaptive difficulty, real-time diagnostics | Most teacher-recommended practice tool |
| **Duolingo** | Streaks, bite-size lessons, wager/commitment mechanics. 7-day streak users 2.4x more likely to return | Retention engineering gold standard |
| **Khan Academy Kids** | Free, mastery paths, playful characters | Baseline expectation |

## Gaps ranked by expected impact

### G1. Visual-first puzzle mode (ST Math insight) - HIGH
Our visuals illustrate the question; ST Math's visuals ARE the question.
A puzzle mode where kids manipulate/choose among visual representations
(drag-equivalent: tap the correct bar model, complete the number bond,
balance the scale) builds conceptual understanding before symbols.
Feasible with existing lib-mathviz: render 3-4 candidate models, ask
"which model shows 3/4?" or "which bar shows 25+17?".
→ CR candidate: `visual-puzzle` question_type.

### G2. Moment-of-misconception feedback (Zearn/DreamBox) - HIGH
Current explanation shows the formula. Best-in-class: the visual model
ANIMATES or re-renders to show why the right answer is right and the
chosen wrong answer fails. Cheaper version: explanation includes a
correct-model SVG + one line diagnosing the likely misconception per
distractor (our distractors are generated from known error patterns -
map each distractor to its misconception text).
→ CR candidate: distractor-aware feedback.

### G3. Strategy-aware adaptivity (DreamBox) - MEDIUM-HIGH
We adapt on right/wrong only. Upgrade: record answer TIME + attempts;
fast+correct = fluency (push ahead), slow+correct = developing, wrong
pattern = misconception (route to remediation skill). Attempt table
already stores answers - needs response-time capture + a selection
signal upgrade in fetch_questions_adaptive.
→ CR candidate: fluency signals in adaptive engine.

### G4. Streak + daily-goal retention loop (Duolingo) - MEDIUM-HIGH
Self-practice dies without habit. Streak counter + small daily goal
("5 câu mỗi ngày") + streak-save (weekend amulet analog: "nghỉ cuối
tuần không mất chuỗi"). Duolingo's data: 7-day streak = 2.4x retention.
→ CR candidate: streaks on the home hub.

### G5. Productive-struggle puzzle content (Beast Academy) - MEDIUM
Our Nâng cao is harder versions of the same skills. BA shows kids engage
with genuinely novel puzzles (logic, pattern, multi-step) that resist
algorithmic answers. Add a puzzle bank: logic grids, magic squares,
number patterns, Vietnamese context word puzzles.
→ CR candidate: `puzzle` skill family in Nâng cao.

### G6. Comic/narrative wrapper (Beast Academy/Prodigy) - MEDIUM-LOW
Characters + story arc per unit. High production cost, real lift but
largest content investment. Defer until G1-G4 land.
