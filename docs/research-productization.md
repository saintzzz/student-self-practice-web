## 5. Risks recorded for later phases

- GS unit wordlists are public in secondary sources (HEID, VietJack,
  VUS, TKBooks) but the OFFICIAL publisher wordlist PDF should be
  obtained during CR-07 BA to verify counts (citation per word, CR-15).
- ~700 new words across G1+3+4+5 need emoji assignment + image
  eligibility review - the existing pipeline scales, but curation time
  is the real cost; plan a maintainer pass per grade.
- Grade 1 UI may need bigger targets + reduced text (CR-09 designer
  should spec the G1 skin as part of the design system).
